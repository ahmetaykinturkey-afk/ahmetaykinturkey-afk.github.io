Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

MatchPath is a mobile puzzle game provided by Ahmet Aykın. Contact: ahmetaykin.turkey@gmail.com.

Game saves

The game stores your best score, sound and vibration preferences, and whether the introductory tutorial has been shown using local storage on your device. No game account is required. Resetting the high score does not reset the tutorial preference. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data; device backups may retain app data.

Optional rewarded advertising

MatchPath integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Where available, completing an eligible ad gives 30 extra seconds once per game after a round ends without a new record.

Google’s advertising SDK may process IP addresses or coarse location, device identifiers, advertising impressions and interactions, and diagnostic and performance information for advertising, measurement, security, and SDK operation. Collection depends on the SDK configuration, device permissions, region, and privacy choices. Ad-related network activity can occur when the game checks consent or loads an ad before you choose to watch one.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Privacy choices and permissions

Where required, the consent provider displays privacy messages. When required, you can reopen the privacy options through Settings → Privacy choices. Where the app requests Apple’s tracking permission, you can allow or deny it and change your choice in iOS Settings. Ordinary play is not conditional on tracking permission or watching an ad.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device model, and issue description. Do not send passwords or payment details. Support communications are used to respond to your request.

Retention, recipients and transfers

Local game preferences remain until reset, removed, or replaced on your device. Google determines retention and processing locations for the data handled by its services, as described in its policies, and may process data outside your country. Support messages are retained only as needed for support and applicable obligations. MatchPath does not provide its own cloud account or player database.

Children’s privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support message, contact us so we can review and address the request. Advertising and consent requirements depend on the user’s region, age where applicable, and the configuration of the advertising services.

Your requests

You can manage device permissions and local data on your device, use the privacy choices provided in the app where available, or contact ahmetaykin.turkey@gmail.com with privacy questions and requests under applicable law. We may need limited information to identify a support conversation; we do not have an account system to look up local scores.

This website

This support website does not include our own advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the game or its advertising features change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
